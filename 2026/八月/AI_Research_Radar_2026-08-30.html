Executive Summary

今天真正值得带走的变化：Agent Unlearning已经从“删除一个entry/skill”快速转向三种closure：行为可重建性（Daydreaming）、source-to-sink workflow resurrection（OBLIVION）、continued post-training influence echo（When Unlearning Fails）。这三类failure分别发生在黑盒行为、环境工作流、未来训练闭环。

Machine Unlearning侧，Scientific Claim Unlearning再次显示：只对一个表面形式做forget，不等于claim-level knowledge真正删除。

Tier S

Daydreaming

Full paper

人话版

不读SKILL.md、不看private trace，只用普通任务最终输出，通过“构造能区分候选规则的任务→看victim结果→更新假设”循环重建hidden skill。Output-only最高恢复86.8%原skill capability，中位约32次victim calls。

Method

public card → property hypotheses → discriminating tasks → victim outputs → shadow candidates → per-file refinement → held-out behavioral evaluation

Results

  • Output最高86.8%，Trace87.0%，Differential86.0%。
  • 中位31–33 calls/skill。
  • query budget约几十次后趋于饱和。
  • 结构恢复很弱但functional utility很高。

Migration

Output-Only Skill Unlearning Recovery：10–20 targets，B=16/32/64；Metric=NU、ASR@budget、Median Calls、Retain FPR。

Safety Does Not Compose

Full paper

人话版

单trajectory guard会因为session reset看不到跨轮fragmented evidence；理论上可退化到TPR=FPR。简单risk decay也只提供固定cooldown。完整outer persistent-state方案把97%级ASR压到约0.1%。

Migration

Composable Continual Agent Unlearning：persistent deletion ledger，比较session-local、decay、latched deletion state。

SARA: Separating Action Induction from Runtime Authorization

Full paper

人话版

Observation诱导动作与动作被授权执行是两层状态。SARA保存action-origin provenance并独立做goal/evidence/argument authorization。GPT/Gemini主setting ASR从15–33%降到≤0.63%。

Migration

Suppression-vs-Unlearning Diagnostic：同时测latent induction和final manifestation，定义Guard Dependence Gap。

Tier A

OBLIVION: Workflow-Level Operational Skill Unlearning for Deployed Agents

Full paper

人话版：skill registry entry删了以后，archive、transcript、vector memory、filesystem、MCP schema仍可能把能力重新拼回来。OBLIVION将攻击表示成residual source→transforms→dangerous sink，并用CSCE跨六类surface清理，再用ARG传播revoked-source taint。

主结果：No-defense ASR=1.000；Static ARG=.750；OBLIVION=.114，IWE=.115，locked utility=1.000，benign block=0。独立sandbox ASR1.0→.2。Task Shield虽ASR0但locked utility全失败；ClawGuard utility1.0但ASR=.455。

意义：这是非常直接的Agent Unlearning Deletion Closure baseline。Worth Deep Reading: YES。

When Unlearning Fails: Reliable Data Deletion under Post-Training in Agent Networks

Full paper

人话版：self-improving agent会继续采集由当前policy生成的新trajectory，forget data的影响因此进入“retained”数据并在后续训练中回流。MUTE用Influence Provenance、NPO+retain、trajectory quarantine、future-update projection与audit scheduling保持删除。

指标：BLI测当前leakage；IRR测未来regeneration；SR测utility；Comm测额外上传。

结果：MiniVLA trajectory/spatial MUTE SR=.777、BLI=.513、IRR=.085、Comm≈53.4；Retrain=.801/.551/.178/234.6。π0 trajectory/object IRR=.062，Comm≈96.9 vs Retrain512.8。

意义：把post-unlearning residual扩展成future echo。Worth Deep Reading: YES。

Scientific Claim Unlearning

Full paper

每个claim生成MCQ/T-F/fill-blank/assertion-reason多种QA并拆成F1/F2两个不重合view。结果显示GD/NPO常强压训练view，却对互补forget view泛化不足；例如OLMo LoRA CS中GD(F1)把F1 R-F1从97.54降到71.70，但F2仍93.51。非常适合做cross-view residual audit。Worth Deep Reading: YES。

Idea Mining

1. Three-Layer Agent Deletion Closure

统一Behavioral Reconstruction（Daydreaming）+ Workflow Resurrection（OBLIVION）+ Future Influence Regeneration（MUTE）。MVE：10 targets×3 deletion methods×3 audit layers。Metric：ASR@budget、Workflow ASR/IWE、IRR、Retain Utility。Novelty ★★★★★ · Feasibility ★★★★☆。

2. Future-Echo Hardness Predictor

用base-stage GPWR/behavioral identifiability/provenance density预测未来IRR，而不是静态post-unlearn leakage。50 targets×6 future rounds；Metric=Spearman/AUROC(high-IRR)。Novelty ★★★★★。

3. Claim-View Residual Auditor

对一个target生成MCQ/T-F/cloze/assertion-reason/counterfactual/multi-hop多view，定义View Transfer Matrix与Worst-View Leakage。Novelty ★★★★☆。

Collision Detection

⚠️ OBLIVION已经明确占据“Operational Skill Unlearning / residual carriers / source-to-sink workflow”；Daydreaming占Output-only functional reconstruction；Agentic Unlearning占parameter-memory pathways。新工作不能只说“删skill还会恢复”。真正剩余gap是统一多层closure、future echo、cross-modal/cross-agent传播和black-box future-IRR prediction。
